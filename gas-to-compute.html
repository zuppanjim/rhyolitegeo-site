What could go wrong

The risks, and how the plan handles each one.

The gas runs out
A shale well can decline 60 to 70 percent in its first year, and a 20-year contract needs firm supply. We pool producers, add a pipeline backstop, and offer geothermal for the long term.
The basis spread closes
New Permian takeaway may lift Waha. The pitch is speed to power, and the target basins reach well beyond the Permian.
Texas grid rules
Under SB 6 the PUCT can curtail a grid-tied co-located load. We design sites as true islands at N+2, or outside ERCOT.
Fiber
Oilfield sites are often fiber-poor. Fiber is screened in step one, and the target is training and batch inference, which tolerate latency.
Engine queues
Reciprocating-engine backlogs hit records at three makers after Q3. We reserve slots or source used and mobile fleets early.
No buyer at exit
No site advances past screening without a named buyer letter.
